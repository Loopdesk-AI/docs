title: Credits and billing
description: 'How Loopdesk uses credits, and where to see your usage, plan and transactions.'
---

Loopdesk's AI features use credits. You can see your credit usage, plan and transactions under **Settings** > **Billing & Usage**.

## What uses credits

| Feature | How credits are used |
|---|---|
| [Storyteller](/storyteller/overview) | Each film is billed by stage, for example for look previews, keyframes, animation, and voice-over and music. |
| [Aura](/video-editor/aura) | Aura actions, such as creating an edit plan, can use credits. |

The number of credits a task uses varies from run to run.

## Billing & Usage

Go to **Settings** > **Billing & Usage** to see:

- your credit usage
- your plan details
- your transaction history
- options for managing your plan and credits

## Questions about billing

For questions about your plan, a charge or your credits, [contact support](/help/contact-support).
