---

**Settings** is at the bottom of the main sidebar. It has three tabs.

## Profile

**Profile** shows your account details and lets you set your timezone.

## Billing & Usage

**Billing & Usage** shows your credit usage, plan details and transaction history, along with options for managing your plan and credits. See [Credits and billing](/account/credits-and-billing).

## Support

**Support** is where you get help from the Loopdesk team and community. See [Contact support](/help/contact-support).
