You can find all of Loopdesk's support options in the app. Go to **Settings** > **Support**.

## Support options

| Option | Use it to |
|---|---|
| Email support | Contact the Loopdesk team directly at [support@loopdesk.ai](mailto:support@loopdesk.ai). |
| Issue form | Report a problem or bug from **Settings** > **Support** in the app. |
| [Discord community](https://discord.gg/ujvTfBvuPg) | Ask questions and talk to other Loopdesk users. |
| FAQs | Find quick answers to common questions in the app, or in the [FAQ](/help/faq). |

## When you report a problem

To help the team solve it quickly, include:

- what you were trying to do
- what you expected to happen
- what happened instead, including the exact wording of any error messages
- the name of the project or film involved
- screenshots or a screen recording, if you can
