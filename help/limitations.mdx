## Storyteller

- A Storyteller film doesn't open directly as an editable Video Editor project. To edit one, download it, then import it into a Video Editor project. See [Edit a Storyteller film](/storyteller/your-films#edit-a-storyteller-film-in-the-video-editor).
- Credit costs and generation times vary from film to film.

## Exporting

- Keep the Loopdesk tab open while your video renders and until your download is complete. Minimizing the window is safe.

## Social accounts and scheduling

- YouTube is currently the only social platform you can connect.
- Meta is listed but not currently available.
- X (Twitter) and TikTok are coming soon.
- After a scheduled post's time has passed, check your YouTube channel to confirm the video was published as expected.

## Cloud storage

- Dropbox connections don't always work as expected at the moment. See [Cloud storage](/cloud-storage/overview).

## General

- Loopdesk runs in your web browser and needs an internet connection.
