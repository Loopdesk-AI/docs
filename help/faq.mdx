## Storyteller

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where can I find films I generated before?">
    Select **Agent** in the main sidebar and scroll to **Your stories** at the bottom of the Storyteller page. Your previous stories are listed with their titles and dates, and you can play and download finished films from there. See [Your stories](/storyteller/your-films).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why doesn't my Storyteller film open in the Video Editor?">
    Storyteller and the Video Editor are separate workflows. A Storyteller film doesn't open directly as an editable Video Editor project.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I edit a Storyteller film?">
    Download the film from **Your stories**, upload it to the **Media Library** in a Video Editor project, then add it to the timeline and edit it like any other clip. See [Edit a Storyteller film](/storyteller/your-films#edit-a-storyteller-film-in-the-video-editor).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does Approval Required mean?">
    Some Storyteller stages are marked **Approval Required**. Storyteller doesn't continue past those stages until you review and approve its work. Not every stage needs approval. See [Generation stages](/storyteller/generation-stages#approvals).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My film seems stuck. What should I check?">
    Check whether the current stage is marked **Approval Required**. If it is, review and approve it so Storyteller can continue.

    If the stage doesn't need approval, or the film still isn't progressing after you approve it, [contact support](/help/contact-support). Include the story's title and the stage it's on.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why do Storyteller costs and generation times vary?">
    Each film is different, and its credits are billed by stage, so both the cost and the time to generate vary from film to film. Check your credits under **Settings** > **Billing & Usage** before you start. See [Generation stages](/storyteller/generation-stages#credits).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Video Editor

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I select, move, trim, split and delete clips?">
    - **Select:** click the clip.
    - **Move:** drag it along its track.
    - **Trim:** drag the beginning or end of the clip.
    - **Split:** move the playhead to where you want to split, then select the scissors control.
    - **Delete:** right-click the clip and select **Delete**.

    See [Work with the timeline](/video-editor/timeline#edit-clips).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I undo or redo a change?">
    Use the undo and redo controls at the left of the timeline toolbar. Undo reverses your most recent change, and redo reapplies it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What are the timeline tracks, and why can't I see all of them?">
    The five standard tracks, from top to bottom, are **Captions**, **Text**, **Motion**, **Video** and **Audio**. Tracks appear according to the content in your project, so a project that uses only captions, text and video shows only those three. The timeline expands as you add content, and you can scroll to reach tracks outside the current view. See [Tracks](/video-editor/timeline#tracks).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What's the difference between playback speed, zoom and Fit to Timeline?">
    - **Playback speed** (the **1x** selector) changes how fast your video plays. It doesn't change the timeline view.
    - **Zoom** (the magnifying-glass **−** and **+** controls and the slider) changes how much of the timeline you see.
    - **Fit to Timeline** (the icon that looks like a fullscreen button) fits all your timeline content into view.

    The timeline has no fullscreen mode. See [Timeline toolbar](/video-editor/timeline#timeline-toolbar).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I change a video clip's position, size, volume or opacity?">
    Use the clip's Video properties panel. It has controls for position, size, rotation, volume, opacity and more. See [Video properties](/video-editor/clip-properties#video-properties).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I add an effect or animation to a video clip?">
    In the Video properties panel, expand **Effect** to choose an effect such as **Zoom**, **Pan** or **Ken Burns** and adjust its settings and timing. Expand **Animation** to set separate **Enter** and **Exit** animations. See [Effects](/video-editor/clip-properties#effects) and [Animation](/video-editor/clip-properties#animation).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title='Why does the captions panel say "No caption at current position"?'>
    There's no caption at the playhead's current position. It isn't an error. Move the playhead to a point that has a caption. See [Captions properties](/video-editor/clip-properties#captions-properties).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Aura

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How does Analyze Video work?">
    Select **Analyze Video**, or ask Aura to analyze your video. Aura may show a processing state at first. When the analysis is ready, select **Start Analysis**. When it's complete, Aura shows a structured result with insights about your video. Results and timing depend on your video. See [Analyze Video](/video-editor/aura#analyze-video).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How does Generate Highlights work?">
    Select **Generate Highlights**, or ask Aura to find the most engaging moments in your video. Aura presents a highlight-reel proposal for you to review. Then choose **Apply Changes** to go ahead, or **Discard** to cancel. See [Generate Highlights](/video-editor/aura#generate-highlights).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How is Suggest Edits different from Generate Highlights?">
    **Generate Highlights** proposes one highlight reel built from your video's most engaging moments, which you apply or discard as a whole. **Suggest Edits** creates an edit plan of individual suggestions, grouped by category and labeled by priority. You choose which ones to apply. See [Suggest edits with Aura](/video-editor/aura-suggest-edits).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does Transcribe do?">
    **Transcribe** is an Aura Quick Action that generates captions for your video. See [Transcribe](/video-editor/aura#transcribe).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I ask Aura something that isn't a Quick Action?">
    Yes. Type your request in the prompt field at the bottom of the Aura panel. You can select a clip on the timeline first to give Aura context.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Exporting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I open the Output Inspector and start an export?">
    In the Video Editor, select **Export** at the top right. The **Output Inspector** opens on the right. Choose your settings, then select **Start Export** at the bottom. When rendering finishes, download your video. See [Export a video](/video-editor/export-video).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which export settings can I change?">
    - **Format:** MP4, WebM, MKV or MOV
    - **Video encoding:** H.264, H.265, VP8, VP9 or ProRes
    - **Quality (CRF):** a value from 0 to 51
    - **Resolution:** 720p, 1080p, 1440p or 4K
    - **Encoding speed:** Ultrafast to Very Slow
    - **Audio codec:** AAC or MP3
    - **Color space:** Default, BT.709 or BT.2020 NCL
    - **Audio:** include it or leave it out

    See [Export settings](/video-editor/export-settings).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does the estimated file size mean?">
    Before you render, the Output Inspector estimates your video's resolution, frame rate, file size and duration from your settings. Use it to check the effect of your settings. For example, a lower CRF value generally gives higher quality and a larger file. See [Export settings](/video-editor/export-settings).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My exported file is too large. What can I change?">
    Try a higher CRF value (toward 51) or a lower resolution, and compare the estimated file size in the **Output Inspector** after each change, before you export. If you don't need ProRes, H.264 or H.265 are alternatives to consider. The size you end up with depends on your video and settings. See [Export settings](/video-editor/export-settings).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I export in 4K?">
    Yes. You can export at 720p, 1080p, 1440p or 4K.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which file formats can I export to?">
    MP4, WebM, MKV and MOV. For most uses, MP4 with H.264 is the most compatible choice.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I minimize the window while my video renders?">
    Yes. Your video renders in the cloud, so it's safe to minimize the window. Keep the Loopdesk tab open until your download is complete.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Scheduling and social accounts

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My scheduled video doesn't appear on YouTube. What should I check?">
    We're still improving scheduling, so check these first:

    1. Open **Calendar** and check the post's status.
    2. Open **Social Accounts** and check that your channel is listed under **Active Nodes** with a successful connection.
    3. Check your channel in YouTube Studio.

    If the video still isn't there, [contact support](/help/contact-support) with the post's title and its scheduled date and time.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My YouTube account doesn't appear when I schedule a post">
    Make sure your channel is connected. Open **Social Accounts** and check that it's listed under **Active Nodes** with a successful connection. If it isn't, [connect your YouTube channel](/social-accounts/connect-youtube).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My video doesn't appear when I schedule a post">
    Scheduling uses rendered videos. [Export your video](/video-editor/export-video) first, then look for it on the **Projects** tab of the **Import Media Source** dialog.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title='Why does Meta sign-in show "App not active"?'>
    Meta connections aren't currently available, so signing in with Facebook shows this message. YouTube is the only platform you can connect right now. See [Social Accounts overview](/social-accounts/overview).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which social platforms can I connect?">
    Currently, YouTube. Meta is listed but isn't available yet, and X (Twitter) and TikTok are coming soon.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Cloud storage

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Dropbox isn't working. What should I do?">
    Dropbox connections don't always work as expected at the moment. Upload your files to the **Media Library** instead, and [contact support](/help/contact-support) to report the problem. See [Cloud storage](/cloud-storage/overview).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Account and support

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where can I see my credits?">
    Go to **Settings** > **Billing & Usage**. In the Video Editor, you can also click the clock in the top bar to show your credits. See [Credits and billing](/account/credits-and-billing).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I get help or report a bug?">
    Go to **Settings** > **Support** in the app, or email [support@loopdesk.ai](mailto:support@loopdesk.ai). When you report a bug, include what you were doing, what you expected, what happened instead, any error messages and screenshots if you can. See [Contact support](/help/contact-support).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
