## About this roadmap

This roadmap is a plain-language tour of everything that landed in Loopdesk
between **December 2025 and October 2026**. It is written for creators, not
engineers: it describes what each feature does for you and how to use it, with
no setup, infrastructure, or behind-the-scenes detail.

Milestones are grouped by quarter and listed in the order they shipped. If you
are looking for a quick version history instead, see [Releases](/releases).

---

## At a glance

Over these ten months Loopdesk grew from a cloud video editor into a full
agentic creation studio. The headline themes:

- **AI that creates and edits for you** — from natural-language timeline edits
  and AI edit suggestions to the new **Storyteller** agent that turns a single
  prompt into a finished, narrated video.
- **Captions in any language** — AI transcription across 100+ languages, a large
  library of caption styles, and first-class support for Indian languages
  (including typing in English and getting native script back).
- **A faster, friendlier timeline** — rebuilt for smooth playback, organized
  rows, progressive clip previews, keyboard shortcuts, and precise on-canvas
  selection.
- **A smarter media library** — upload once and work from anywhere, search your
  footage by filename *or* by what actually happens on screen, and pull in
  royalty-free stock and music without leaving the editor.
- **Effortless export** — background cloud rendering up to 4K with live progress,
  so you can close the tab and come back to a finished video.
- **Plan, schedule, and publish** — a built-in calendar and scheduler for
  Instagram, Facebook, and YouTube.
- **Clear, flexible pricing** — subscriptions, a card-required free trial,
  pay-as-you-go credits, lifetime access, and live credit visibility right in
  the editor.

## Q4 2025 — Foundations in the cloud

### December 2025 · Unified design & a smarter AI chat

- **Unified design language** — a consistent look, typography, and navigation

- **Rebuilt timeline** — completely re-engineered for smoother interactions,
  better performance, and a cleaner look.

- **Sidebar redesign** — Transcript and Show Notes moved to the left sidebar for

## Q1 2026 — The editor comes alive

### January 2026 · Motion library, chat redesign & keyboard shortcuts

- **Motion library** — browse and drop in animated overlays such as geometric
  wipes, infographics, stickers, and GIFs from a new Motion panel.
- **Redesigned AI chat** — a fresh look with a better conversation flow and a
  loading indicator when starting new chats.
- **Keyboard shortcuts** — full shortcut support, including J/K/L playback
  control and frame-by-frame arrow navigation. Press `?` to open a searchable
  shortcuts dialog.
- **Friendlier errors** — helpful recovery screens with retry and back options
  instead of a blank page, plus a new panel to choose export format, codec, and
  quality.

### January 2026 · Video effects, typography & adaptive preview

- **Video effects** — apply zoom, pan, and Ken Burns moves to any video or image,
  fine-tuned with simple controls.
- **Advanced typography** — hundreds of fonts plus letter-spacing, line-height,
  and advanced styling for captions and text overlays.
- **Adaptive preview** — the preview area resizes dynamically to make the best
  use of your screen.
- **Smarter, faster AI** — more accurate video understanding with streaming
  analysis that shows results as they arrive, plus faster exports and
  pixel-perfect text sizing.

### January 2026 · Cloud media library

- **Cloud media library** — your library is fully cloud-based: upload once and
  reach your files from any device.
- **Unified media selector** — browse project files, your cloud library, and
  stock media from a single panel.
- **Smart downloads** — real-time export progress with automatic retries if
  something goes wrong, plus clearer track organization.

### February 2026 · AI timeline editing & social login

- **AI timeline editing** — tell the assistant what you want in plain English
  ("trim the first 5 seconds," "split at 1:30," "delete the gap") and it edits
  the timeline for you, including split, trim, delete, ripple delete, slip, and
  slide.
- **Onboarding tour** — new users get a guided walkthrough of the editor's key
  features.
- **Facebook & Instagram login** — sign in with your Meta account alongside
  Google and email.
- **Natural chat & audio extraction** — slash commands are gone (just type
  naturally), you can pull the audio out of any clip, and the download button
  shows live progress.

### February 2026 · Subscriptions, billing & activity feed

- **Subscriptions & billing** — choose a Creator plan with captioning minutes,
  storage limits, and pay-as-you-go credits, all managed from a new billing
  settings page.
- **Redesigned pricing page** — a clear breakdown of each plan with one-click
  upgrades and downgrades.
- **Activity feed** — a live feed of everything happening in your account:
  uploads, exports, plan changes, and more.
- **Profile, privacy & terms** — your profile shows your join date and plan, with
  dedicated Privacy Policy and Terms of Service pages.

### February 2026 · Interactive highlights & smart silence removal

- **Interactive highlights** — AI-detected highlights appear directly on the
  timeline; click any one to jump straight to that moment.
- **Smart silence removal** — automatically find and remove silent stretches,
  with presets (such as Aggressive or Gentle) to match your editing style, while
  keeping intentional pauses and reaction shots.
- **Account linking** — if you signed up with both email and a social login, your
  accounts merge automatically so you never lose a project.
- **Smoother timeline zoom** — zooming now anchors to your cursor for more
  precise navigation.

### March 2026 · Performance, scheduling & smarter placement

- **Faster media loading & login refresh** — media loads noticeably faster, the
  login screen has a fresh look, and silence removal is more reliable.
- **A more capable assistant** — the AI assistant understands your videos better
  and responds faster.
- **Interactive scheduler** — plan and publish content to Instagram, Facebook,
  and YouTube from an interactive calendar with full event management.
- **Smart overlay placement** — text is placed collision-free so overlays don't
  stack on top of each other, and audio lands on its own row automatically.
- **Timeline & caption polish** — smoother timeline performance and improved
  caption rendering.

## Q2 2026 — Text, captions & intelligent media

### April 2026 · A bigger type & caption toolkit

- **Expanded text overlays** — the text system grew to 6 categories and 79 fonts
  so you can find the right look fast.
- **Multilingual caption engine** — broader, more accurate transcription with
  automatic detection of international scripts and support for 8 Indian language
  scripts: Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, Bengali, Gujarati, and
  Punjabi.
- **Font system overhaul & more free styles** — a refreshed font system, an
  expanded caption-template collection, and many caption styles that were
  previously Pro now available to everyone.

### April 2026 · Account & upload improvements

- **Email verification** — email sign-ups are now verified for added account
  security.
- **Better support tickets** — support requests are saved and routed reliably so
  nothing falls through the cracks.
- **Hardened uploads** — a more dependable upload pipeline with a queue, saved
  progress, drag-and-drop, and live sync status so large uploads just work.

### April–May 2026 · Cloud rendering & the upload tray

- **Cloud rendering improvements** — a more robust export system for faster,
  more reliable renders.
- **Unified upload tray** — all upload progress is consolidated into a single,
  tidy tray instead of scattered indicators.

### May 2026 · AI timeline tools & scene search

- **Audio cleanup** — an AI tool that cleans up your audio directly on the
  timeline.
- **Highlight reel** — automatically assemble the best moments of your footage
  into a ready-to-share reel.
- **Scene search groundwork** — the foundation for searching your footage by what
  actually happens on screen, not just by filename.

### June 2026 · Search your footage & flexible credits

- **Media library search** — filter by filename and search *semantically* by
  scene, so you can find "the moment someone opens the door" without scrubbing.
- **Multilingual, tag-driven search** — library search understands multiple
  languages and tags for faster discovery.
- **Free trial, redesigned** — a 3-day, card-required trial with a capped budget
  that converts automatically when it ends.
- **Smarter credit spending** — AI actions spend your subscription credits first
  and fall back to pay-as-you-go credits automatically, so the assistant keeps
  working even after your monthly allowance runs out, with clear per-action
  pricing.

- **Smarter edit suggestions** — the assistant now proposes grounded, ready-to-

## Q4 2026 — The Storyteller era

### October 2026 · Performance & reliability

- **Platform reliability** — behind-the-scenes improvements that make the app
  faster and more dependable across the board.

### October 2026 · Storyteller — one prompt, a finished video

**Storyteller** is Loopdesk's AI video agent: describe the video you want and it
produces a complete, narrated film for you.

- **Start from a prompt** — type what you want and, optionally, attach up to four
  reference photos or videos.
- **Choose the format** — vertical (9:16) for Reels, Shorts, and TikTok, or
  landscape (16:9).
- **Pick a length** — from a quick clip (under 10 seconds) to long-form (up to 5
  minutes).
- **Add a voiceover** — choose a male or female narrator and the narration
  language.
- **Stay in control** — review and approve each step before any credits are
  spent.
- **Runs in the background** — close the tab and come back later; your finished
  film is waiting on its run page, visible only to you.
