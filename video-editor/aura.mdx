Aura is the AI assistant in the Video Editor. You can use a **Quick Action** for a common task, or ask Aura anything in your own words.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/Aura textbox.png" alt="The Aura panel, showing the Quick Actions Analyze Video, Generate Highlights, Suggest Edits and Transcribe, and the prompt field at the bottom" />
</Frame>

## Quick Actions

| Quick Action | What it does | Learn more |
|---|---|---|
| **Analyze Video** | Gives you AI insights about your video's content. | [Analyze Video](#analyze-video) |
| **Generate Highlights** | Finds the most engaging moments in your video and proposes a highlight reel. | [Generate Highlights](#generate-highlights) |
| **Suggest Edits** | Creates an edit plan of suggestions that you can review and apply. | [Suggest edits with Aura](/video-editor/aura-suggest-edits) |
| **Transcribe** | Generates captions for your video. | [Transcribe](#transcribe) |

## Ask Aura in your own words

Type a request or question in the prompt field at the bottom of the panel. As the field suggests, you can select a clip on the timeline first to give Aura context about it.

## Analyze Video

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request an analysis">
    Select the **Analyze Video** Quick Action, or ask Aura to analyze your video.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the analysis">
    Aura may show a processing state at first. When the analysis is ready, select **Start Analysis**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/video analyse Aura.png" alt="Aura showing a Video Analysis card in the Ready state, with a Start Analysis button" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the results">
    When the analysis is complete, Aura shows a structured result with insights about your video.
  </Step>
</Steps>

How long an analysis takes, and what it covers, depends on your video.

## Generate Highlights

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask for highlights">
    Select the **Generate Highlights** Quick Action, or ask Aura to find the most engaging moments in your video.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the proposal">
    Aura presents a highlight-reel proposal that describes what it will do. The proposal also shows an estimated time and an expected impact. These are estimates, not guarantees.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/HIghlights aura.png" alt="Aura showing a highlight-reel proposal with an estimate, an impact level, and Discard and Apply Changes buttons" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply or discard it">
    Select **Apply Changes** to go ahead, or **Discard** to cancel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Suggest Edits

Aura reviews your video and creates an edit plan. You choose which suggestions to apply. See [Suggest edits with Aura](/video-editor/aura-suggest-edits).

## Transcribe

Select the **Transcribe** Quick Action to generate captions for your video.

Captions sit on the **Captions** track of the [timeline](/video-editor/timeline). To work with them, see [Captions properties](/video-editor/clip-properties#captions-properties).

## Clean up silences

Aura can find silent sections in your audio and help you remove them. For each silent section it finds, you can choose:

- **KEEP** to leave the section in your video
- **REMOVE** to cut it out

To remove every silent section at once, select **Remove Silences**. To clear your choices and start again, use the reset option.

<Tip>
  Review each section before you remove it. A pause can be deliberate, for example for emphasis or before a punchline.
</Tip>

## Conversation history

Aura keeps a history of your conversations. Expand **History** at the top of the panel to see your previous conversations, or select **New** to start a new one.

## Credits

Aura actions can use credits, and the amount varies from run to run. To check your credits, go to **Settings** > **Billing & Usage**.
