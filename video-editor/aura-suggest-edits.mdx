description: 'Get an AI edit plan for your video and apply the suggestions you want to your timeline.'

Aura's **Suggest Edits** action reviews your video and creates an edit plan, which is a list of suggested changes. You choose which suggestions to keep, then apply them to your timeline.

## Get and apply suggestions

<Steps>
  <Step title="Run Suggest Edits">
    In Aura, select the **Suggest Edits** Quick Action, or ask Aura to suggest edits. Aura creates an **Edit plan** for your video.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the edit plan">
    At the top of the edit plan, you'll see:

    - how many suggestions it contains
    - **Signals**, the sources Aura analyzed to make its suggestions, such as your transcript and audio regions
    - filter buttons, so you can view all suggestions, only high-priority ones or one category at a time
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review each suggestion">
    Suggestions are grouped by category, for example **Pacing**, **Typography** and **Engagement**. Each suggestion shows:

    - a **priority** label, such as **high** or **medium**, to help you decide which suggestions matter most
    - the part of your video it applies to, and a short description of the change
    - a **Why** section that you can expand to read Aura's reasoning
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the suggestions you want">
    Select the checkbox next to each suggestion that you want to apply.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add them to your timeline">
    Select **Add to Timeline**. Aura applies the selected suggestions to your timeline and marks them as **Applied**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Read the **Why** explanation before you apply a suggestion, so you can judge whether it suits your video.
</Tip>

## Credits

Each edit plan uses credits, and the amount can differ from run to run. When a run finishes, Aura shows how many credits it used. To check your credits, go to **Settings** > **Billing & Usage**.
