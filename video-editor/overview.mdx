The Video Editor is where you build a video yourself. You arrange media, text and motion elements on a timeline, get help from Aura, the editor's AI assistant, and export the finished video.

<Note>
  The Video Editor is separate from [Storyteller](/storyteller/overview). A Storyteller film doesn't open directly as an editor project, but you can download it and import it into a project to edit it. See [Edit a Storyteller film](/storyteller/your-films#edit-a-storyteller-film-in-the-video-editor).
</Note>

## Create or open a project

### Create a project

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new project">
    Select **Projects** in the main sidebar, then select **Create Project**. The **Create New Project** dialog opens.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/Create project.png" alt="The Create New Project dialog, with a Project Name field, an Aspect Ratio menu set to Landscape (16:9) — YouTube, and the Create Project button" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your project">
    Enter a name for your project.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an aspect ratio">
    Choose the aspect ratio that suits where your video will be shown:

    - **Landscape (16:9)** — YouTube
    - **Portrait (9:16)** — TikTok, Reels
    - **Square (1:1)** — Instagram
    - **Vertical (4:5)** — Social

    You can change the aspect ratio later in the editor, using the aspect-ratio selector on the [timeline](/video-editor/timeline).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the project">
    Select **Create Project**. Your new project opens in the editor.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Open an existing project

Select **Projects** in the main sidebar, then select the project's card. The project opens in the editor.

## Parts of the editor

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/Editor.png" alt="The Video Editor, with the Media Library panel on the left, the preview canvas in the center, the timeline below it and the Export button at the top right" />
</Frame>

| Area | What it's for | Learn more |
|---|---|---|
| **Media Library** | Upload, search and filter your own media. | [Add media](/video-editor/add-media) |
| **Stock Media** | Browse stock video, audio and images. | [Add media](/video-editor/add-media#stock-media) |
| **Text** | Add styled text from a range of categories. | [Text and motion](/video-editor/text-and-motion) |
| **Motion** | Browse emoji and motion presets. | [Text and motion](/video-editor/text-and-motion#motion) |
| **Timeline** | Arrange clips on the Captions, Text, Motion, Video and Audio tracks, edit them and play back your video. | [Work with the timeline](/video-editor/timeline) |
| **Properties panel** | Adjust the selected clip, including video effects, animations, captions and text formatting. | [Clip properties](/video-editor/clip-properties) |
| **Aura** | Ask the AI assistant to analyze your video, suggest edits, create highlights, generate captions or clean up silences. | [Aura AI assistant](/video-editor/aura) |
| **Output Inspector** | Choose export settings and render your video. | [Export a video](/video-editor/export-video) |

## Top bar

The bar across the top of the editor shows your project's details and gives you quick access to editor tools.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/top editor bar(time).png" alt="The top bar of the editor, showing the side-panel toggle, the project title, the aspect ratio and duration, the clock display, and the shortcuts, notifications, activity log and Export controls" />
</Frame>

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| Side-panel toggle | Shows or hides the side panel. It's at the far left. |
| Project title | The name of your project. |
| Aspect ratio and duration | The project's aspect ratio and how long the video is. |
| Clock display | Shows the time. Click it to switch to your credits display. |
| Keyboard shortcuts | Opens a map of the editor's keyboard shortcuts. |
| Notifications | Shows your notifications. A number badge can appear on the bell icon. |
| Activity log | Shows recent activity. |
| **Export** | Opens the **Output Inspector** so you can export your video. See [Export a video](/video-editor/export-video). |

When you click the clock, the same spot shows your credits instead:

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/Top editor bar(credits).png" alt="The top bar of the editor with the credits display shown in place of the clock" />
</Frame>

The time and credit figures in these screenshots are examples. Your top bar shows your own values.

## A typical editing workflow

1. Upload your footage to the **Media Library**, or find clips in **Stock Media**.
2. Arrange your clips on the timeline.
3. Add text, captions and motion elements.
4. Fine-tune each clip, such as its position, volume, effects and animations, in its properties panel. See [Clip properties](/video-editor/clip-properties).
5. Use Aura to suggest edits, generate captions or remove silences.
6. Export your video from the **Output Inspector** and download it.
