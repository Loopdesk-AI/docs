Use **Text** and **Motion** in the editor to add titles, captions, emoji and animated elements to your video.

## Text

**Text** offers styled text in these categories:

- Essentials
- Trending
- Effects
- Cinematic
- Social & Captions
- Lifestyle & Script
- Indian Languages

Browse a category to find a style that fits your video.

## Motion

**Motion** lets you browse:

- **Emoji** to add to your video
- **Presets** for motion elements

## On the timeline

Text elements sit on the **Text** track, and motion elements sit on the **Motion** track. See [Work with the timeline](/video-editor/timeline).

To change a text element's content, font, size, alignment or colors, use its properties panel. See [Text properties](/video-editor/clip-properties#text-properties).

## Related

- To generate captions from your video's speech, use Aura's **Transcribe** action. See [Aura AI assistant](/video-editor/aura).
