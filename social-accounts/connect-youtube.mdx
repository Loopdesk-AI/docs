---
title: Connect a YouTube channel
description: 'Connect your YouTube channel so you can schedule posts and view analytics.'
---

Connect your YouTube channel to schedule posts to it from the **Calendar** and to see its performance in **Analytics**.

## Before you begin

Make sure you can sign in to the Google account that manages your YouTube channel.

## Connect your channel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Social Accounts">
    Select **Social Accounts** in the main sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose YouTube">
    Under **Connect Node**, choose **YouTube**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with Google">
    Sign in with the Google account for your YouTube channel and follow the prompts to give Loopdesk access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the connection">
    Your channel appears under **Active Nodes**. Check that its status shows a successful connection.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you can do next

- [Schedule a post](/scheduling/schedule-a-post) to your channel.
- View your channel's performance in [Channel Insights](/analytics/channel-insights).
