---
title: Social Accounts overview
description: 'Connect social platforms and cloud storage, and view statistics for your account.'
---

**Social Accounts** is where you connect the social platforms you schedule posts to and view analytics for. It also holds your cloud storage connections and account statistics. To open it, select **Social Accounts** in the main sidebar.

## Sections

| Section | What it contains |
|---|---|
| **Active Nodes** | Your connected social accounts and the status of each connection. |
| **Connect Node** | The platforms you can connect. |
| **Storage Accounts** | Cloud storage providers. See [Cloud storage](/cloud-storage/overview). |
| **Statistics** | A summary of your account activity. |

## Supported platforms

| Platform | Status |
|---|---|
| YouTube | Available. See [Connect a YouTube channel](/social-accounts/connect-youtube). |
| Meta | Listed, but not currently available. Signing in with Facebook shows an "App not active" message. |
| X (Twitter) | Coming soon. |
| TikTok | Coming soon. |

## Statistics

**Statistics** shows:

- connected accounts
- storage used
- total posts
- scheduled posts
- published posts
- the date you became a member
