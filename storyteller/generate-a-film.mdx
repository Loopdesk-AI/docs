This guide shows you how to start a Storyteller film and see it through to the end.

## Before you begin

- Make sure you have enough credits. To check your credits, go to **Settings** > **Billing & Usage**.
- Leave yourself time to review the film. Storyteller pauses at any stage marked **Approval Required** and continues only after you approve it.

## Start a film

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Storyteller">
    Select **Agent** in the main sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe your film">
    Write a prompt that describes the film you want. Include the subject, the story or message, the audience and the tone.

    You can also choose one of the [presets](/storyteller/overview#presets).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your settings">
    Set the aspect ratio, duration, language, accent, voice gender and captions. See [Film settings](#film-settings) below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Improve your prompt (optional)">
    Use the prompt improvement option if you want Storyteller to refine your prompt before you submit it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit your prompt">
    Submit the prompt to start generating your film.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and approve each stage">
    Storyteller works through each stage of the film. When a stage is marked **Approval Required**, review the work and approve it so Storyteller can continue. See [Generation stages](/storyteller/generation-stages).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open your finished film">
    When the film is assembled, you can play and download it from **Your stories**, at the bottom of the Storyteller page. See [Your stories](/storyteller/your-films).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Film settings

| Setting | What it controls |
|---|---|
| Aspect ratio | The shape of the film's frame. |
| Duration | How long the film is. |
| Language | The language of the film. |
| Accent | The accent of the narrator's voice. |
| Voice gender | The gender of the narrator's voice. |
| Captions | Whether the film has captions. |
| Prompt improvement | Whether Storyteller refines your prompt before generating. |

The options for each setting appear in Storyteller when you create a film.

## Tips for writing a prompt

- **Be specific.** Name the subject, the setting and the key points the film should cover.
- **Describe the style.** Mention the mood or visual style you want, for example cinematic, documentary or playful.
- **Say who it's for.** A film for customers needs a different tone from a film for students.
