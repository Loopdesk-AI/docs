Your previous Storyteller stories are listed in **Your stories**.

## Find your stories

1. Select **Agent** in the main sidebar to open Storyteller.
2. Scroll to **Your stories** at the bottom of the page.

Each story shows its title and the date and time it was created. Some stories also show a film count.

</Frame>

## Play a film

When a story has produced a film, you can play the film in your browser's video player.

## Download a film

Select **Download** on the film you want. The video file downloads to your computer.

## Edit a Storyteller film in the Video Editor

A Storyteller film doesn't open directly as an editable Video Editor project. To edit it, download it and import it into a project:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download the film">
    In **Your stories**, select **Download** on the finished film.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a project in the Video Editor">
    Open an existing project, or create a new one. See [Create or open a project](/video-editor/overview#create-or-open-a-project).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import the film">
    Upload the downloaded video to the **Media Library**. See [Add media](/video-editor/add-media).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit it on the timeline">
    Add the video to the timeline and edit it like any other clip. See [Work with the timeline](/video-editor/timeline).
  </Step>
</Steps>

To get a different result from Storyteller itself, start a new story with an updated prompt or different settings.
