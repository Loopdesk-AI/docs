The **Calendar** shows your scheduled posts and lets you schedule rendered videos to a connected social account. To open it, select **Calendar** in the main sidebar.

<Note>
  To schedule posts, you need a connected account. Currently, YouTube is the only platform you can connect. See [Connect a YouTube channel](/social-accounts/connect-youtube).
</Note>

## Calendar views

| View | Shows |
|---|---|
| Day | A single day. |
| Week | A full week. |
| Month | A full month. |
| Timeline | Your scheduled posts along a timeline. |
| Agenda | Your scheduled posts as a list. |

## Other calendar features

- **Timezone selector:** Changes the timezone used to display times on the calendar. The scheduling dialog also shows the timezone you select.
- **AI-recommended posting windows:** The calendar suggests times to post.
- **Drag to Schedule:** A sidebar that lists your projects.
- **Post counts:** The calendar shows how many posts are upcoming and how many are done.

## Scheduled posts on the calendar

Each scheduled post appears on the calendar with:

- the video's name
- the platform it's scheduled to, such as YouTube
- its current status
- a **Pause** control
