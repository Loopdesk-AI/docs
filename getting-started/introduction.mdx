Loopdesk is a web app for making and publishing video. You can generate a complete film from a written prompt, edit your own footage with help from an AI assistant, schedule finished videos to a connected YouTube channel and review your channel's performance.

## Storyteller and the Video Editor are separate

Loopdesk has two separate ways to create a video:

- **Storyteller** generates a finished film from your prompt. You review and approve its work along the way, then play or download the result. A Storyteller film doesn't open directly in the Video Editor, but you can download it and import it into a project to edit it.
- The **Video Editor** is where you build and edit a project yourself on a timeline, with Aura to help.

To choose the right one for your video, see [How Loopdesk works](/getting-started/workflow-overview).

## Credits

Some AI features use credits, including Storyteller and Aura. The amount depends on the task. To check your balance and usage, go to **Settings** > **Billing & Usage**. See [Credits and billing](/account/credits-and-billing).
