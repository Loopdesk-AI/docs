Loopdesk runs in your web browser at [app.loopdesk.ai](https://app.loopdesk.ai). After you sign in, the main sidebar takes you to each area of the app.

## Main sidebar

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/project page.png" alt="The My Projects page in Loopdesk, with the main sidebar on the left, project cards, the project search box and the Create Project button" />
</Frame>

| Sidebar item | What you do there | Learn more |
|---|---|---|
| **Projects** | Browse your projects as cards, search for a project, select a project's card to open it, or select **Create Project** to start a new one. | [Create or open a project](/video-editor/overview#create-or-open-a-project) |
| **Agent** | Open Storyteller to generate a film from a prompt. | [Storyteller overview](/storyteller/overview) |
| **Calendar** | View scheduled posts and schedule rendered videos to a connected account. | [Calendar](/scheduling/calendar) |
| **Analytics** | View performance data for a connected YouTube channel. | [Channel Insights](/analytics/channel-insights) |
| **Content Library** | Open the **Asset Library** to browse files across your connected storage. | [Cloud storage](/cloud-storage/overview) |
| **Social Accounts** | Connect social platforms and cloud storage, and view account statistics. | [Social Accounts overview](/social-accounts/overview) |

## Settings

**Settings** is at the bottom of the sidebar. It has three tabs:

| Tab | What it contains | Learn more |
|---|---|---|
| **Profile** | Your account details and timezone setting. | [Account settings](/account/settings) |
| **Billing & Usage** | Credit usage, plan details, transaction history and options for managing your plan and credits. | [Credits and billing](/account/credits-and-billing) |
| **Support** | The Discord community, email support, an issue form, FAQs and a link to documentation. | [Contact support](/help/contact-support) |
